# Global AI resume / CV improvement tools (as of Oct 2026): feature and pricing profile vs CVmax

Research method note: official pricing pages were fetched directly where possible (Teal, Rezi, Kickresume, Huntr, Novoresume, Zety, Resume Worded Pro, Enhancv, Canva, Resume Worded homepage and LLM-info page). Jobscan and Careerflow pricing pages could not be fetched (blocked / 404), so their prices come from multiple 2026 third-party reviews that agree. **Bias warning:** almost every "review" or "comparison" found is published by a competing AI-job tool (Wobo, FastApply, Resume Optimizer Pro, JobSprout, CVSmith, Careery, LoopCV, Careerkit, Remote Job Assistant, OwlApply, HAIRED, ApplyMate, Qwyse, Resume Genius, Kickresume itself, Teal itself). Prices are usually checkable, but verdicts and "we tested" claims are self-interested. No neutral, non-vendor 2026 comparison was found. Prices are USD unless noted; some vendors (Enhancv, Canva, Novoresume) localize prices by region and run price cohorts.

## Q1. Per-tool profile: core features, free tier, paid price

### Takeaway
Paid tiers cluster at **~$24–$50/month billed monthly** (cheaper on 3/6/12-month billing: ~$8–$30/month effective). Every tool has a free tier, but it is usually a demo: limited AI credits (Teal 10 bullets), limited scans (Jobscan 5/month), limited downloads (Rezi 3 PDFs ever), watermark/7-day window (Enhancv), or a TXT-only export with an auto-renewing $1.95 trial (Zety). Kickresume is the only major tool found that gives **full Premium free to verified students**.

### Inferences
- The realistic price anchor a student sees is **$24–$50 per month**, or ~$8–$20/month only with long prepayment. A genuinely free, unlimited-download, JD-specific analysis tool (CVmax) is therefore a meaningful differentiator for students on Ukrainian budgets; Kickresume's free-for-students offer is the closest equivalent.
- Several builders (Zety, Resume.io, MyPerfectResume, Resume Now) monetize via cheap auto-renewing trials; "free" in this market usually means "free to build, pay to download".

### Gaps
- Jobscan and Careerflow official pricing pages could not be fetched; figures rely on third-party reviews dated Mar–Sep 2026 that agree with each other.

- Interview prep, multi-language and versioning for Zety, Novoresume and Canva were not documented in sources found.
- No source quantified total "templates" for Rezi Pro vs free precisely beyond "20+" / "46 CV designs" conflicts.

## Q2. Table stakes vs differentiators

### Takeaway
Table stakes in 2026: a resume builder with ATS-safe templates, an ATS/quality score, JD keyword matching/gap list, AI bullet/summary writing, AI cover letter, PDF export, and LinkedIn/PDF import. Differentiators are: job tracker + Chrome extension + autofill (Teal, Huntr, Simplify, Jobright), LinkedIn profile review (Resume Worded, Careerflow, Jobscan), AI mock interviews (Rezi, Careerflow Plus), auto-apply (Jobscan since Jun 2026, Jobright), human expert review (Rezi Pro), and design/translation (Kickresume, Enhancv).

### Cited Findings
- JD keyword matching / score is offered by Teal, Rezi, Jobscan, Huntr, Resume Worded, Enhancv, Careerflow. — [Teal pricing](https://www.tealhq.com/pricing); [Rezi pricing](https://www.rezi.ai/pricing); [CVSmith](https://www.cvsmith.app/blog/jobscan-review); [Huntr pricing](https://www.huntr.co/pricing); [Resume Worded](https://resumeworded.com); [OwlApply Enhancv](https://owlapply.com/en/blog/enhancv-review); [Wobo Careerflow](https://www.wobo.ai/blog/careerflow-review)
- AI cover letters are present in Teal, Rezi, Kickresume, Enhancv, Resume Worded, Jobscan, Huntr, Careerflow, Zety. — same sources as above plus [Zety pricing](https://zety.com/pricing); [Resume Worded LLM info](https://resumeworded.com/llm-info)
- Job trackers / Chrome extensions: Teal (unlimited, free), Huntr (100 jobs free), Jobscan (free tracker + extension), Careerflow (10 free), Enhancv (AI Job Application Tracker), Simplify, Jobright. — [Teal](https://www.tealhq.com/pricing); [Huntr](https://www.huntr.co/pricing); [CVSmith](https://www.cvsmith.app/blog/jobscan-review); [Wobo Careerflow](https://www.wobo.ai/blog/careerflow-review); [Enhancv](https://enhancv.com/pricing/); [CVSmith trackers](https://www.cvsmith.app/blog/best-job-application-trackers)
- Interview prep exists only in some: Rezi AI Interview, Careerflow Premium Plus mock interview ($44.99/mo), Kickresume interview-question generator, Huntr question generator, Jobscan discounted add-on; Teal has none. — [ATS Resume AI](https://www.atsresumeai.com/compare/rezi-review); [Wobo Careerflow](https://www.wobo.ai/blog/careerflow-review); [FastApply Kickresume](https://blog.fastapply.co/fastapply-vs-kickresume-review-and-comparison-2026); [Huntr](https://huntr.co); [CVSmith](https://www.cvsmith.app/blog/jobscan-review); [ResuFit](https://resufit.com/blog/teal-resume-builder-how-ai-technology-is-revolutionizing-resume-creation)
- Line-by-line review with approve-each-change and clarifying questions is offered by Resume Worded AutoFix (closest analogue to CVmax's per-line verdicts + "Grill me" + accept one by one). — [Resume Worded](https://resumeworded.com)
- Skill-gap / career direction: Kickresume Career Map (salary insights, skills-gap analysis) and AI Career Coach action plan; Rezi AI Skills Explorer; Jobright/Huntr job matching. — [PitchMeAI](https://pitchmeai.framer.website/blog/kickresume-pricing-features-guide); [Kickresume help center](https://www.kickresume.com/en/help-center/best-ai-resume-builders); [Rezi blog](https://www.rezi.ai/posts/rezi-for-career-changers)
- Translation: Enhancv (9 languages) and Kickresume (Resume Translation) translate resumes; no tool found offering feedback in Ukrainian. — [Enhancv](https://enhancv.com/pricing/); [FastApply Kickresume](https://blog.fastapply.co/fastapply-vs-kickresume-review-and-comparison-2026)

### Inferences
- What competitors offer that CVmax lacks: cover letter generation, job tracker / Chrome extension / autofill, LinkedIn profile optimization, mock interview practice for the actual job interview (CVmax's "Grill me" is for CV content, not interview rehearsal), multiple templates/design options, resume translation, mobile apps, human expert review, auto-apply.
- Where CVmax is distinct (based on what was found): (1) explicit per-line verdict taxonomy (keep/cut/shorten/rewrite/move) — only Resume Worded's line-by-line engine/AutoFix is comparable, and it's paid ($19–49/mo); (2) a dedicated fact checker flagging invented tools/numbers — no competitor advertises this as a feature; (3) per-academic-program rubrics (economics, law, psychology, etc.) — no competitor found does this; (4) Ukrainian-language feedback; (5) analysis against company type, not just JD; (6) completely free for its target users.
- CVmax's "what to learn" and "where to apply" overlap with Kickresume Career Map / AI Career Coach and Rezi Skills Explorer, so they are not unique, but they are paywalled elsewhere.

### Gaps
- Could not verify whether any tool offers a separate "fact check" or "no-fabrication mode" toggle; none was found in official pages.
- No evidence found on whether Teal, Jobscan or Rezi support non-English feedback/UI.

### Inferences
- CVmax's university-pilot model (free, single institution, program rubrics) resembles the B2B "career center" channel used by Jobscan/Huntr/Resume Worded, but those are generic US-centric tools; CVmax's localization (Ukrainian feedback, KSE programs, international-internship framing for Ukrainian students) is a niche none of them serve.

### Gaps
- No pricing found for university/institutional licenses of Jobscan, Huntr, or Resume Worded.
- No evidence found of any of these tools having Ukrainian-language UI or feedback.

## Q4. What tools do about hallucinated / exaggerated content

### Takeaway
No major tool advertises a fact-checking or anti-fabrication feature. Reviewers document that several AI writers (Rezi, Kickresume) generate generic bullets and invented numbers not tied to the user's history; Rezi even shipped "ATS Hack Mode" (invisible keywords, since June 2024). Resume Worded's 2026 AutoFix is the only one found that explicitly markets "rewrites from your own experience" and asks the user for missing numbers before inserting them. The common vendor answer is "AI is a first draft — fact-check it yourself."

### Cited Findings
- Rezi: in a competitor's test, it "generated a generic bullet point with specific numbers that was not connected to the test resume". — [Qwyse comparison](https://www.qwyse.com/blog/best-ai-resume-builders) (competitor)
- Rezi: users report "no-op rewrites and outright hallucinations"; "ATS Hack Mode" (June 2024) injects invisible keywords — white-text stuffing, flagged as unethical by a Trustpilot reviewer; reviewer says headline claims (4.3M users, 62.18% interview rate) don't hold up. — [Wobo Rezi review](https://www.wobo.ai/blog/rezi-review) (competitor)
- Kickresume AI Writer output "generated from job title, not your history; wordy, generic". — [Wobo Kickresume review](https://www.wobo.ai/blog/kickresume-review) (competitor)
- General: "you still need to fact-check the output, since AI can invent metrics or generic phrasing. Treat it as a fast first draft". — [ApplyMate](https://apply-mate.com/blog/best-ai-resume-builders) (competitor)
- Critique of the category: popular tailoring tools (Rezi, Huntr, Teal, Enhancv, Simplify) "invent projects you never worked on. Fabricate metrics you never hit. Add skills you've never touched." — [Sid Saladi, The Product Channel](https://sidsaladi.substack.com/p/build-your-own-ai-resume-tailor-a) (opinion, newsletter author promoting own alternative)
- Teal marketing acknowledges "AI will hallucinate and make up metrics and outcomes" and pitches its builder for "perfectly placed metrics". — [LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/renoperry_9-ai-prompts-that-transform-weak-resumes-activity-7382404092431818752-qmoz) (snippet only; could not verify full context)
- Resume Worded AutoFix: "drafts the rewrite for every weak line from your own experience, asks what it needs to know, and you approve each change"; demo asks "how many client reports did you automate?" before writing the number. — [Resume Worded](https://resumeworded.com)
- Hiring-side context: 49% of US hiring managers say they automatically dismiss resumes identified as AI-generated (Resume.io survey of 3,000, Jan 2025); 62% reject AI resumes lacking personalization (Resume Now, n=925, Mar 2025); 74% have encountered AI content (Resume Genius, n=1,000, Jan 2025) — all vendor-run surveys, pre-2026. — [JobCannon stats roundup](https://jobcannon.io/blog/ai-resume-statistics-2026)
- Robert Half (2,000+ US hiring managers, Nov 2025, published Mar 2026): 65% say AI-enhanced applications made skills harder to verify; 38% added more interviews per candidate; no mention of AI-detection software. TopResume (n=600, May 2025): 19.6% would reject a candidate with an AI-generated resume. No published survey measures how many employers run AI detectors on resumes. — [Resume Optimizer Pro AI detector guide](https://resumeoptimizerpro.com/blog/ai-resume-detector-guide)
- Jobscan's own position: ATS doesn't reject resumes for using AI; rejections come from generic unedited content; recruiters reject generic AI writing "on sight". — [Jobscan blog](https://www.jobscan.co/blog/can-ats-detect-ai-resume)

### Inferences
- CVmax's fact checker (flagging invented tools/numbers) plus "Grill me" (eliciting real numbers from the user) directly addresses the most-cited weakness of the category; only Resume Worded AutoFix's clarifying-question flow is comparable, and none advertise a post-hoc fabrication check. This is a credible positioning point ("honest CV", "no invented metrics") — especially for students whose CVs are thin and who are most tempted to inflate.
- The hiring-manager surveys are pre-2026 and vendor-run; use as directional evidence only.

### Gaps
- No official policy statements on fabrication were found from Teal, Rezi, Kickresume, Enhancv, Jobscan, or Huntr.
- Independent (non-competitor) testing of hallucination rates across tools was not found.
- Survey data on AI detection is 2025 and mostly from resume-builder vendors (possibly outdated / biased).
